# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version
2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The short
version, which is the part that gets used:

Be decent to people. Assume the person you are replying to is trying, and reply to
what they wrote rather than to the worst reading of it. Disagreement about a
technical decision is welcome and is most of what a project like this is for;
contempt for a person is not.

Harassment, insults, and unwelcome attention are out of bounds here whether or not
they arrive dressed as a technical argument.

This project also ranks places where real people live, on measures including
depression, life expectancy and self-rated health. Discussion of those numbers
stays about the numbers and their limits. A county is not its statistics, and
nobody is obliged to defend where they live.

## Reporting

Raise it with the maintainer, **[@krimsonkla](https://github.com/krimsonkla)** — by
opening an issue where that is appropriate, or privately through GitHub if it is
not. Reports will be read and handled by one person, in confidence.

The maintainer may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, code, issues and pull
requests that do not belong here, and will say when they have.
